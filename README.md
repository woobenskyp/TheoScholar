### TheoScholar
## A Theological Research Library

TheoScholar is an online theological research library for searching and exploring Christian primary sources, from the writings of the Church Fathers and medieval theologians to the Reformers, councils, confessions, sermons, letters, and other foundational works. It provides a comprehensive environment for theological research, bringing together powerful search, reading, organization, annotation, citation, and other tools for the entire research workflow.

[Visit TheoScholar](https://www.theoscholar.org)
